Wednesday (Sept 23<sup>rd</sup> 2026) I crashed on my bed sometime in the evening around 6 PM and woke up next morning around 8 AM. I woke up an hour before
midnight to eat dinner that I gobbled up in just two minutes while standing at the kitchen counter. I wanted to go back to sleep before my sleepiness went away.
I am a light sleeper so I always fear that once I wake up I can not go back to sleep. I had never slept so much in my entire life ever before.
I was fatigued. The last four days had been extremely weary, I was exploring Sri Lanka with friends and somehow it drained me to my core. Comparing it to my
other travels, I mostly just rested on the beach, swam in pools every day and had plenty of sleep, yet somehow I was more tired than I was when I rode
my bike every day for excruciating hours over a fortnight. Maybe I am getting older or maybe it's all in my head. I think it was the traffic that I had to endure on the
way back from the airport. Flights exhaust me in general. All the sitting and waiting and getting back my luggage and the commute is the end of me. But I was knocked out on the flight
back as well, so I guess the trip itself was tiring for me.  
![A beach with sunset and coconut tree on the left](https://theblog-static.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/week24/IMG_0857.webp "My stay in Sri Lanka was quite literally on the beach. I got to see the sea quite a lot")
A majority of my waking hours in Sri Lanka were spent in the water. I was either in a pool or at the beach.
I have been to beaches before a countless times but for the first time I was dressed appropriately for it, fashioned in the mighty swimsuit. It rained during my visit which made enjoying the shore difficult, but on the last day the clouds made way for the sun. I buried my legs in a mound of sand and lied down to enjoy the sun. _T_ was sitting beside me and every time I looked over, the sun gleamed beautifully over her. At that moment I thought that things are not bad after all. Usually I complain about things a lot, a lot in my head, a lot less to others
but then moments like these allow me to remind myself that yeah I should stop that.  
Other time was mostly spent around in a car not enough for a group of 5 travelling around Galle. On our first night we found a taxi driver, who spoke Hindi and we hired him for the next two days. He seemed excited to show us around stopping at places unprompted and telling explain the context behind them. Anyway, most people that we encountered did not understand
English, that is when we tried to speak in English. Weirdly it was hard to register for us that we are in a foreign land and no one understands Hindi. Maybe it was because we thought
the people looked like us or maybe it was the many tuk-tuks against the backdrop of small unremarkable shops beside the roads much like the shops back home. Whatever it was I, and others, often found ourselves trying to communicate in Hindi with the expectation of being understood.
![](https://theblog-static.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/week24/IMG_0877.webp "On our last day in Galle we finally found a good place to eat at, by the sea shore")

There was a lot more that I did, and there was a lot I did not get to do. Like always I do not wish to write about it all. I had fun and brought back memories that I'll hopefully get to cherish for a long time to come. That's important.

## Away, for a while
I have travelled much in the last few months, all of them with _T_. A few weeks before I went to Sri Lanka we drove to Ooty, on a whim. That was a whole ordeal. I wanted to stay at a
quiet place, away from everything that goes on in Bangalore. I skimmed through listings of stays around Ooty and picked the one that had a nice view from the window, a bonfire and a barbeque. That's everything I need to enjoy my weekend in peace. I rented an electric car to travel to Ooty. I had never driven an electric car before so I spent the entire trip looking at the battery capacity, calculating how much more I can actually travel and whether I'll make it to the stay if I skip the one charging station next to me and don't find one later.
I decided not to gamble and stopped at every charging station I could find but even that was pointless. Either they did not work or I could not work them. I finally charged it at the very last station before travelling another 150KMs to my destination where I would find the next one. Only because there was another car charging which meant it worked even though I could not figure out how. It is likely I would have skipped that station if not for the other car there charging, which in retrospect would have been a catastrophe since from that point on reaching Ooty took more battery than what my car was left with had I not charged.

Ooty was a strange place. It was beautiful but at the same time I would probably not stay there. Luckily the sky was clear, and I was staying 20KMs away from Ooty at my nice stay where
I got to enjoy a very beautiful sunset from my room. Later I drove around in the town to look for something to eat and to get some beer. I found neither of those and decided to head
back and sit around my bonfire ("My" because I had to pay 2K extra for it. Make it make sense). At night it was pleasantly cold with chilly winds which made sitting around the
bonfire very enjoyable.
Time always slows down when I am away from Bangalore. We sat at the bonfire for two hours while I played with the fire wood which felt like a long time. Like longer than I can spare,
as if I had something important to do. It always feels wrong to be doing nothing for all that time, like I could have done something else. Gone for a walk maybe. To realize that what
felt like an eternity was only a couple of hours makes me long for more days where I can slow down and sit in quiet and the only sound is the crackle of burning wood and chirp of crickets.

## Where has been Anirudh?
I know the blog does not really serve its purpose if I don't write. I was not too busy to write. I had the time. I think I lacked the motivation, as in I did not know what
I should write about, though I wanted to write.

In my free time I.. I guess I don't do much. I have a lot of things to work on. I wish to leave India for good, sooner than later. I want to stop living under a rock. Here and there
are small things that I wish I did better but in the last few months I just come home from work and then spent time with my girlfriend. I have no complaints about that, though somewhere I lose the balance between all the things. I easily obsess over one thing and everything else takes a backseat. I feel I need to fix that somehow. Learn to find balance between things I care about and things I wish for.
