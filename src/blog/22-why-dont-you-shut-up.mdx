I often wonder if I am doing good. The question is not about my well being, most of the time
I am doing good in that sense. It is more akin to the quality of my life holistically. It is
one of the things ask myself often and do not know how to approach it. What does it mean to be doing
good? What is the threshold of good meter. When do I unlock my batman outfit for doing good?
Is there an easier way, like a book? 101 Tips To Figure Out How Much You Suck. Is that what everyone
else is reading and no one has told me or do they think of similar things. I feel troubled not knowing
answers to these questions. I feel my life will soon derail if I don't figure out the answer to
this seemingly impossible question and it will be up there with the likes of "Is hotdog a sandwich?".
That troubles me too.

Through the different stages of my life I have had people who have believed in me in the sense that
they think I am capable of doing good. When I think about that I wonder what do they know about me that I don't.
Is there something they know about life in a much more profound sense and I fail to see it? I do feel
I should have taken them seriously, tried to understand why they feel the way the feel about me. What
should have I done differently to be the person they think I am capable of or am I that person but so blinded
by doubt that I'll never see it. Sometimes I think I am so confused because I never had a person who
could help me navigate these challenges. Maybe one must learn how to trust oneself trough the experiences
of others. I still have people who tell me that I do good or that I am capable of doing good and it's
hard to agree with them. I just can not make myself agree with them because there are also people who
tell me that I am no good or that I am incapable of doing good. I care about what they have to say more
than all these people who have tried to tell me otherwise. I don't know why. One of them is not better
than the other. It's impossible to believe that someone thinks I'll be fine but it is very convincing
that I'm bound to fuck up. The problem is listening to one or the other still does not answer my question
of whether I am doing good. I just now have less will to figure out the answer. I have reached the state
where I am fine believing that actually I can't do good and I hate it because it holds me back. A million
arms reach up from the ground and hold my feet down before I can even think of doing something. If I do
it well is not even a question because I believe it will be bad. I hate it. I hate being held back so
you know what, fuck you. Maybe I'll never know if I am doing good but if I listen to the people who tell
me I'll do good at least I will be able to move my feet. So for the rest of you all, why don't you just shut up?

On a different and less existential topic, I celebrated my birthday (I know one person for whom
this is more existential). What I mean is unlike like the last 10 years I actually thought about it, decided
to celebrate and invited people over to spend some time with me on the occasion, and got upset when
they were late. I know right, insane stuff. I am surprised at myself because I somewhat convinced myself
to celebrate only because _T_ had been counting the days down to me and I thought to myself, for someone so excited
about my birthday, I need to celebrate it so that I can invite them. Other thing that convinced me was the fact that
I'll get to drink booze with my friends at home and get Roomate to meet them. I was worried that it will be an awkward
event but I had fun. Cheers to getting a year closer to chronic joint pain.

Shitya was in Bangalore the same week so I had to had to meet her. Our birthday's are next to each
others so we sometimes send each other birthday gifts, it's like a small tradition (at least i think)
that we follow only sometimes. It made me happy to do it this time personally. Grateful to have you
as a friend, I know you will probably never read this dumbass.
